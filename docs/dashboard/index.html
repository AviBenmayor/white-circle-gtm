How the score works

The top 50

Each company can earn up to 90 points: 80 for being able to buy, and up to 10 for matching the markets White Circle already sells to. Missing data scores zero rather than counting against a company. Region adds no points; it only breaks ties. The points are starting assumptions, to be tuned once real meetings and wins come in.

The AE list

Top 50 accounts

How this list is built
  • Gates first: closed, acquired, non-profit, public, competitor, services firm, dead website, no language-AI product, industry not matched.
  • Then the points: recent funding 40, revenue 25, team size 15.
  • Fit bonus up to 10, from White Circle's own landing pages and messaging.
  • Region (Europe and North America) breaks ties only.
  • Every top-50 company is verified against its live website.
  • The points are starting assumptions, recalibrated against real results and rescored periodically.
